Trigger card: variations

Every variant draws the same three triggers in the 300 px Entity inspector: Coin pickup (a check and two actions), Timed door (a Wait between two actions, a Repeat limit) and Jump pad (switched off, with an unset check). Colours and font are the editor's tokens; the coloured parts (amber When, teal If, blue Do, violet Wait) are new and only used where a variant says so.

Part 1 is how one trigger reads. Part 2 is how the list of triggers is laid out, and works with any Part 1 look. Pick one from each. Rows marked as clickable open and close; menus are not wired up.
