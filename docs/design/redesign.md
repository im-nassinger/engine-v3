# The redesign

## TL;DR

The owner wants a new look for the editor before v2 starts (2026-11-17): **clean, cool and beautiful, with no borders, separator lines or outlines**, even if it looks less "professional" than commercial tools. Only the look changes (colours, spacing, layout, the parts' shapes); what the screen shows stays. **Settled so far:** the Islands look, the owner's set of parts, panels holding only their own content, no level name, no View button, a 2 px dark shadow under every grey container (29), the panels titled **Entity inspector** and **Entities viewer**, and the layout of 17 to 20: ☰ alone at the top left, holding every command (Undo, Redo, Save and Play included) in its submenus, the tools in a column under it, the Entity inspector beside them from the top, a bare minimap at the bottom right with a debug box under it (24), and Grid snapping in the View menu (18), and a dark level view outside the level bounds with a night example level (25), cards titled without "Component" (26), islands with 14 px corners (27), and the Shape tool's kinds in a menu beside it (28). **Open:** what Save does. The mockup is [mockups/redesign.html](mockups/redesign.html); the [round 8 design](README.md) stays what is built until this one is written into [tokens.md](tokens.md) and [controls.md](controls.md) and planned as a stage.
