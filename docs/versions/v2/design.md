## TL;DR

**v2's screen parts are designed and approved** (owner, 2026-11-20): the pen and the Pencil in the tool column, the Pencil's smoothness menu, a path's points in the Entity inspector, the gradient editor, and the points, handles and gradient handles on the level view.
